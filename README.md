# 🏥 Healthcare Insurance Cost Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes healthcare insurance charges based on patient demographics, medical conditions, lifestyle habits, and hospitalization details. The goal is to identify the key factors that influence medical insurance costs and provide actionable insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Healthcare insurance companies need to understand the factors that drive medical expenses to:

- Improve risk assessment
- Optimize premium pricing
- Identify high-risk customers
- Enhance healthcare planning and resource allocation

This project explores how age, BMI, diabetes status, smoking habits, hospital tier, and other medical attributes impact healthcare charges.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Smoking Significantly Increases Medical Costs
Patients who smoke tend to incur substantially higher healthcare expenses compared to non-smokers.

### 2. Obesity Leads to Higher Charges
Customers classified as obese show increased medical costs due to higher health risks.

### 3. Diabetes Impacts Healthcare Spending
Diabetic patients generally generate higher hospitalization and treatment expenses.

### 4. Age is a Major Cost Driver
Healthcare charges tend to increase as patient age increases.

### 5. Hospital Tier Influences Charges
Premium hospitals (higher tiers) are associated with higher treatment costs.
